# Umami growth review

Use the taxonomy and setup in [analytics.md](analytics.md). In Umami, compare weekly cohorts and filter by device class and display mode. Device identity is browser-local, so cleared storage and separate profiles can undercount returning people.

Review these funnels: activation from the record page through camera permission, start, keep and export; search landing to Studio and a kept take; install prompt to install; and desktop-to-phone handoff through receipt and a kept take. Inspect journeys beginning at `/studio/home` to find common paths and dead ends. Compare the `pwa`, `calendar` and `share_card` UTM sources.

Every week spend 30 minutes on Weekly Recording Creators and DAU/MAU, the largest activation drop by device and browser, camera failure reasons, cohort retention, and the strongest and weakest landing-page conversions. Record one decision in the Studio decision log. Search Console supplies impressions and clicks before the visit; Umami measures behavior after arrival.
